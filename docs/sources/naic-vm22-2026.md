# NAIC Valuation Manual, VM-22 (1 January 2026 edition)

**Source.** *Valuation Manual*, chapter VM-22, "Requirements for
Principle-Based Reserves for Non-Variable Annuities", 1 January 2026
edition. Produced by the **NAIC** (National Association of Insurance
Commissioners); chapter developed by the VM-22 (A) Subgroup under the Life
Insurance and Annuities (A) Committee. No individual author — it is a
regulatory text adopted by member states.

<https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/pbr_data_valuation_manual_current_edition.pdf>

**How the figures here were obtained.** The 457-page PDF was fetched and
its text extracted mechanically (PyMuPDF), and chapter VM-22 isolated from
it. Quotations below are verbatim from that extraction, with the section
numbers the text carries.

## What this checked here, and the result

`engine/report/vm22.py`. **It found three errors**, all now fixed — see
RFC-039's "Corrected against the Valuation Manual" section:

1. **§3.A** — the module had modelled the aggregate reserve as a *maximum*
   over components of one block (VM-20's shape) rather than a *sum* over a
   partition of the book. `AggregateReserve` now sums `ReservingGroup`s.
2. **§4.B.1** — the module floored the CTE *result*; the text floors each
   scenario *before* the CTE. Since `max(F, X)` dominates both arguments
   pointwise and a CTE is monotone, the original ordering **understated**
   the reserve. Fixed, with `floor_outside_reserve` kept so the gap can be
   reported.
3. **§7.C.1** — the module divided by the baseline reserve; the text
   divides by the present value of benefits, a different and larger
   quantity.

Two things the module already had right, now citable rather than assumed:
**CTE 70**, and the refusal to default the exclusion threshold — because
the threshold is a *minimum against a company-specific number*, a bare 6 %
default would have been wrong too. `SERT_CAP = 0.06` is now carried;
`materiality_standard` stays `None`.

## Still open — deviations found, recorded, not yet fixed

A second pass over the chapter (prompted by "have you corrected *all* the
errors?") found two more places where the module knowingly differs from
§4. Both push the reserve **upward**, so the error is conservative rather
than deficient, and both are pinned by tests in `tests/test_vm22.py` so
they cannot be forgotten:

| § | text | module | why not fixed here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.F.5.a.ii | "Combine the present values for each model segment and take the greatest present value **in aggregate** for each scenario" | takes the greatest present value **per contract**, then sums — `Σ max` where the text asks `max Σ` | needs `Contract` to carry the discounted deficiency *path* rather than the reduced scenario reserve |
| 4.B.1.a | guidance note: "The greatest present value of accumulated deficiencies **can be negative**" | floors it at zero (RFC-016: "a *surplus* is not a negative reserve") | the floor is in `engine/report/pbr.py`, shared with VM-20 and VM-21; removing it there would revalue two chapters on a third's text |

Both are the same shape as the floor-placement error already corrected:
**reduce-then-aggregate where the text aggregates-then-reduces**. Three
instances of one mistake is a pattern, and it is evidently the mistake this
framework invites.

Fixed in the same pass: §4.B.1.a's **PIMR** ("the starting asset amount,
less the allocated amount of PIMR, plus the greatest present value…") is
now an argument to `Contract.from_cashflows`, defaulting to zero.

## Completeness

**This is not a systematic read of the chapter.** VM-22 runs to roughly
sixty pages and the figures above were found by keyword search against an
extracted text — CTE, exclusion, cash surrender, aggregate reserve, PIMR.
Sections not searched have not been checked, and the deterministic
reserve (§7.E), the Single Scenario Test, the standard projection method
(§6) and the allocation rules (§13) are among them. Anything this file does
not mention should be assumed unchecked rather than checked and clean.

## What is still not carried

The 16 prescribed economic scenarios (VM-20 Appendix 1.F) and the
prescribed assumption sets. They are dated regulatory data and belong with
execution-plan item F2's regulation-as-dated-sets work, not inlined.
